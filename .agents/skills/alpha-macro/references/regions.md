# Regional coverage

Choose a relevant subset, not an exhaustive recurring checklist.

| Region | Primary starting points | Distinctive analytical checks |
|---|---|---|
| United States | BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve, Treasury, official ISM releases | Payroll revisions; participation versus unemployment; CPI/PCE component differences; control-group retail sales; GDP composition; futures/OIS versus Fed guidance; fiscal supply and funding plumbing. |
| China / Hong Kong | NBS, PBOC, ministries/regulators, HKEX, issuer filings | Official/survey PMI divergence; credit flow versus stock; property completions and local fiscal stress; validate activity proxies. Separate A/H/ADR/VIE access and currency risks. Verify current Stock Connect disclosure availability before claiming live flow granularity. |
| Japan | BOJ, Statistics Bureau, Cabinet Office, METI, JPX | Verify current monetary framework rather than assuming yield-curve control is active. Wages versus real incomes; import-cost versus export translation effects; governance/capital allocation; JPY carry-unwind scenario and sector divergence. |
| Korea | BOK, Statistics Korea, MOTIE, KRX, DART | Export quantities versus prices; semiconductor inventory, DRAM/NAND and HBM mix; won, household debt, corporate governance and shareholder returns; peninsula tail risk. |
| APAC ex-China/Japan/Korea | RBA, RBNZ, RBI, CBC, MAS and national statistics/central banks | Australia/NZ housing, wages and China commodity demand; India macro/credit; Taiwan exports; ASEAN manufacturing, tourism and consumption. MAS uses an exchange-rate framework: distinguish its policy from direct control of domestic interest rates. |
| EMEA / other developed markets | ECB/Eurostat, BoE/ONS, BoC/Statistics Canada, local official sources | Energy and fiscal divergence, real wages, housing/credit transmission, regional sovereign spreads, commodity dependence. Keep sovereign and currency jurisdictions distinct. |
| Emerging markets ex-China | Local central banks/statistics/exchanges, IMF and World Bank | External debt, reserves, current account, dollar sensitivity, capital flow composition, local versus hard-currency returns; India, Brazil and ASEAN equity mechanisms. |

APAC macro and EM equity coverage overlap. Tag country and economic mechanism, and avoid counting the same Indian/ASEAN observation twice when synthesizing.

Central-bank text coverage spans Fed, ECB, BoE, BOJ, PBOC, BOK and relevant regional banks. Identify original language, source date, speaker role and whether a statement reflects a committee decision. Historical 2025/2026 dot plots and interest-rate examples in the source are not current defaults.
