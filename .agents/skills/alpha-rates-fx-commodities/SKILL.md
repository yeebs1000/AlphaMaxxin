# Rates, FX & Commodities

## Establish comparable market inputs

Specify instrument, venue, timestamp, quote convention, tenor, currency and units. Distinguish policy rates, overnight benchmarks, nominal yields, real yields, and forward rates. Use dated curves; do not substitute a spot rate for every maturity.

Analyze yield-curve level/slope/curvature, term-premium hypotheses, issuance and liquidity. Separate spread duration from interest-rate duration and credit spread widening from Treasury moves. For a small yield move, use delta P/P approximately -D_modified * delta y + 0.5 * convexity * delta y squared; delta y is a decimal. Disclose when optionality or a large shock makes that approximation unsuitable.

For funding liquidity, examine reserve balances, repo/overnight rate spreads, collateral conditions and issuance/central-bank balance-sheet effects. Distinguish a temporary calendar or collateral distortion from broad credit stress; proposed stress thresholds need explicit observation windows.

Breakeven inflation = matched-maturity nominal yield minus inflation-linked real yield. Treat it as market compensation influenced by risk/liquidity premia, not a pure forecast. Equity earnings yield minus Treasury yield is a valuation proxy, not a complete expected equity risk premium model.

## FX and commodity transmission

For FX, state base/quote currency and analyze rate differentials, relative growth, external balance, positioning and policy. Distinguish spot appreciation, forward points and realized carry after financing. Examine local-currency and base-currency investment outcomes.

For commodities, analyze physical balance, inventories, marginal cost, capacity, seasonality, geopolitics and term structure. Identify the actual underlying: WTI/Brent, refined jet fuel, grade of industrial metal, gold, or bulk commodity. Map the company's exposures, contract lags and hedges before translating a spot move into earnings.

Separate spot change, futures convergence/roll return, and collateral yield. Contango alone does not establish a profitable short or arbitrage. A front/next-month price difference is not total realized roll cost without contract, timing and price-path assumptions.

## Output

Give a dated market-input table, dominant drivers, scenarios by tenor/currency/commodity, asset-specific directional implications, and principal risks. Keep missing forward curves, credit data and positioning explicit. Funding and broker-specific margin questions belong with alpha-portfolio-risk.


## Evidence and signal contract

Record the asset/venue, currency, analysis timestamp, observation period, source links, and publication/availability dates. Refresh current claims from primary sources when permitted; with supplied-data or offline-only requests, label unverified current claims rather than assuming live verification. Identify estimates and unavailable inputs explicitly. Missing evidence is unavailable, not a neutral score. Source examples and portfolio names are illustrations, not current holdings or facts.

When a directional signal helps, give an asset-specific score from -2 (bearish) to +2 (bullish), confidence High/Medium/Low with a reason, horizon, key risk, and falsification condition. Keep tactical (0–4 weeks), positional (1–6 months), and thematic (6–24 months) assessments separate. Confidence is an evidence assessment, not a probability. Omit a score when evidence is inadequate.
