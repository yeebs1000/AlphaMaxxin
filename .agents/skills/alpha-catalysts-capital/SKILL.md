# Catalysts & Capital Markets

## Build a verified event map

Use issuer, exchange, regulator and central-bank sources. Record event date/time/timezone, announcement date, source, confirmation status, affected assets and mechanism. Label tentative dates and separate anticipated events from confirmed ones. A calendar is a dated snapshot; it does not imply ongoing monitoring.

For earnings, economic releases, trials or product events, separate consensus, market-implied move, scenarios and actual results. A straddle-based move estimate requires contemporaneous quotes, expiry selection and method; it is neither a physical probability nor a guaranteed range. Account for weekends, exchange holidays and publication lags.

An implied move above historical realized event moves is a comparison, not proof of rich options, mispricing or profitable short volatility. Match expiry/event windows, move definitions and sample periods; disclose small samples and changes in forward event risk.

## Capital markets

For IPOs, inspect prospectus/registration documents, use of proceeds, ownership, dilution, free float, stabilization and contractual lockup provisions. Verify actual unlock terms and amendments; do not assume 180 days. For placements, model new shares, price discount, balance-sheet effect and per-share value, not just raised cash.

For M&A and take-privates, distinguish rumor, signed agreement, shareholder/regulatory approval, financing and completion. Calculate consideration and deal spread from current terms, and assess timing, break risk, competing bids and standalone downside. Do not call an uncompleted spread riskless arbitrage.

For LBO/private capital, assess leverage, interest coverage, covenant structure, debt syndication and fund deployment/realization. Stated dry powder is not immediate demand for a specific listed company. NAV proxies require current holdings, valuation dates, fees, liabilities and liquidity/stale-mark adjustments.

For activist campaigns, distinguish disclosed ownership and proposals from board adoption or completed actions. Trace possible asset sales, buybacks, governance changes and capital structure effects through per-share value and execution risk.

## Output

Give a sortable dated event table, thesis mechanism, priced expectation, scenario upside/downside, probability only if justified, confirmation status, and invalidation. Include financing and dilution impacts for corporate actions. Provide follow-up research needs without creating schedules or subscriptions unless requested.


## Evidence and signal contract

Record the asset/venue, currency, analysis timestamp, observation period, source links, and publication/availability dates. Refresh current claims from primary sources when permitted; with supplied-data or offline-only requests, label unverified current claims rather than assuming live verification. Identify estimates and unavailable inputs explicitly. Missing evidence is unavailable, not a neutral score. Source examples and portfolio names are illustrations, not current holdings or facts.

When a directional signal helps, give an asset-specific score from -2 (bearish) to +2 (bullish), confidence High/Medium/Low with a reason, horizon, key risk, and falsification condition. Keep tactical (0–4 weeks), positional (1–6 months), and thematic (6–24 months) assessments separate. Confidence is an evidence assessment, not a probability. Omit a score when evidence is inadequate.
