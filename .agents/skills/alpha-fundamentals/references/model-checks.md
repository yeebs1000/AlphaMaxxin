# Model and accounting checks

- FCFF is discounted at WACC to enterprise value; FCFE is discounted at cost of equity to equity value. Match nominal cash flows to nominal rates and real cash flows to real rates, in the same currency. A TIPS real yield is not a drop-in nominal risk-free rate.
- For a perpetuity terminal value: TV at year n = FCF_(n+1)/(r-g), requiring r>g and a sustainable reinvestment/growth relationship. Discount the terminal value to the valuation date. Quantify its share of value.
- Equity bridge: enterprise value minus debt and debt-like/non-common claims plus relevant nonoperating assets/cash, avoiding double counts; divide by reconciled fully diluted shares. Treat leases and pensions consistently with the cash flows and peer multiples.
- ROIC = after-tax operating profit / consistently defined average invested capital. State tax and capital definitions. Three-part DuPont ROE = net profit margin * asset turnover * equity multiplier, using aligned periods and average balance sheet denominators.
- Reconcile CFO/net income, working-capital seasonality, stock compensation, capitalized costs and nonrecurring items. CFO/NI below one is a screen, not proof of revenue manipulation; loss-making firms need a different interpretation.
- Beneish M-Score versions and cutoff conventions differ. Cite the chosen model, variables and original calibration; test availability and comparability of every input. The source's -1.78 cutoff is not a universal manipulation probability or allegation.
- Modified Jones abnormal accrual estimation requires a meaningful peer/time sample and fitted model. Do not pretend a single-company accrual ratio is the regression model.
- Multiple z-scores require comparable historical definitions and sufficient observations. Corporate regime changes can invalidate a ten-year average. Avoid EV/EBITDA for financial institutions without a justified method.
- A proprietary Quality/Value/Momentum/Low-volatility/Size taxonomy is not the Fama-French five-factor model. Name and source the actual regressors used. Significance of a beta is not exposure magnitude or crowding.
- NAV for private holdings needs valuation dates, dilution, liquidity restrictions, fees, liabilities and stale-mark sensitivity. Never treat a last private funding valuation as realizable fair value.

Illustrative sensitivity ranges from the source (discount rate +/-150 bps, terminal growth +/-100 bps) can be starting scenarios when sensible; user inputs and economic feasibility take precedence.
