# Disclosure and proxy checks

## Politician disclosures

Use official House/Senate disclosure repositories before aggregators. Verify current rules rather than embedding permanent filing deadlines. The House Committee on Ethics explains PTR deadlines as the earlier of 30 days after awareness or 45 days after the transaction; public posting can occur later. Use actual public availability for historical eligibility, not a statutory deadline. Source: https://ethics.house.gov/financial-disclosure/

Retain transaction date, filing date, public availability date, amendment/version, filer, owner (self/spouse/dependent), asset identifier, type, and reported minimum/maximum value. Report a cluster's lower/upper range; a midpoint is an estimate, not a known trade size. Separate disclosures, holdings and actual inferred transactions to prevent duplicates.

Source Prompt 14b requires both fundamental and technical positive signals (+1 to +2) and >$100,000 concurrent accumulation for +0.5. Prompt 23 instead requires political score>1.5 and fundamental>1.0, omitting the technical and amount conditions. Neither variant has demonstrated predictive validity in the document. Record these as alternative research hypotheses, not an activated policy.

## Alternative data

Satellite/logistics: geography, coverage/clouds, processing time, AIS gaps, storage versus throughput, duplicate vessels and seasonal baseline.

Card spending/geolocation: panel composition, merchant mappings, inflation, sample turnover, opt-in bias, privacy-preserving aggregation and observed visits versus transactions.

Hiring/web data: scraping coverage, removed/duplicate job listings, reposting, labor supply, product changes, website instrumentation and conversion to revenue.

Corporate aviation: public flight coverage and identity uncertainty. Travel is compatible with many explanations; do not turn a trip into a deal assertion.

Social data: query drift, platform outages, coordinated posts, multiple languages, repeated news and bots. Compare signal availability with any subsequent price move.
